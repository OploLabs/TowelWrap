How to Play

The Goal

Take turns placing your piece on the board.

Your piece must follow your assigned orientation.

If you have no legal move, you lose. If the board becomes completely full, the game is a tie.

1. Place Your Piece

Each piece covers the configured number of neighboring squares.

Horizontal piece
Neighboring blue squares make one horizontal piece.
Vertical piece
Neighboring red squares make one vertical piece.

2. Take Turns

Players alternate turns. Every square used by your piece must be empty.

Occupied squares are blocked
A piece cannot overlap another piece.

3. Manage Your Clock

Each player has their own clock. Only the current player's clock runs. After a move, the clocks switch.

Stopwatch mode counts upward. Countdown modes can be selected in Settings.

4. Win or Tie

  • No legal move → the configured no-move rule applies.
  • The other player normally wins.
  • Completely full board → the configured full-board rule applies.
  • In countdown mode, reaching zero can end the game.

Computer Players

Computer players use the same legal-move rules as human players. The AI searches simulated board states and never modifies the real board while thinking.

Computer AI

The AI runs entirely inside this HTML file. No network connection, server, API, model download, or external library is required.

Easy uses a shallow search with a short time limit. Normal searches deeper with alpha-beta pruning and caching. Hard uses longer iterative deepening and stronger move ordering. The actual search depth is automatically limited by the browser search time.
