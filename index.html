How to Play

The Goal

Take turns placing your two-cell piece on the board.

Your piece must follow your assigned orientation.

If you have no legal move, you lose. If the board becomes completely full, the game is a tie.

1. Place Your Piece

Each piece covers exactly two neighboring squares.

Horizontal piece
Two neighboring blue squares make one horizontal piece.
Vertical piece
Two neighboring red squares make one vertical piece.

2. Take Turns

Players alternate turns. Both squares used by your piece must be empty.

Occupied squares are blocked
A piece cannot overlap another piece.

3. Win or Tie

  • No legal move → you lose.
  • The other player wins.
  • Completely full board → tie.
